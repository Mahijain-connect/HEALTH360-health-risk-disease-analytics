About HEALTH-360

Understanding healthcare data for better insights.

Project Overview

HEALTH-360 is a healthcare analytics project designed to explore health-related data, identify patterns, and present meaningful insights through interactive visualizations and dashboards.

Our Vision

To make complex healthcare information easier to understand through data analysis and visualization.

Our Objective

To analyze different health conditions and risk factors using data analytics and interactive dashboards.

Key Health Factors

The project explores factors related to Diabetes, Heart Disease, Blood Pressure, Bmi,Fatigue,Age,Lifestyle factors(Alcohol and Smoking).
